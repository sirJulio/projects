planit · Victorian owner-builder pathway

Build cost examples

Summary

  • Three Melbourne builds, each costed three ways: a builder does the entire build, an owner-builder with no industry contacts, and an owner-builder using planit.
  • Using planit lowers the owner-builder total in every example, by $7,809 (Example 1), $57,458 (Example 2) and $126,284 (Example 3) compared with owner-building unaided.
  • Against a builder doing the entire build, the owner-builder total with planit is $10,573 lower in Example 1 and $90,604 lower in Example 3, and $18,931 higher in Example 2.
  • Owner-building without industry contacts costs more than a builder in Example 2 ($76,389 more) and Example 3 ($35,680 more), and $2,764 less in Example 1.
  • Every figure is an estimated total cash cost in AUD including GST. They are estimates, not quotes, and the assumptions behind them are not shown here.
